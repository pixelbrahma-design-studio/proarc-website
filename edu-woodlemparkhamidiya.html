Woodlempark Hamidiya School

Woodlempark Hamidiya School is an educational development delivered by Proarc in Hamidiya, Ajman, spanning a built-up area of 26,000 sqm. Designed to support a modern learning environment, the project balances functional academic spaces with thoughtful architecture, reflecting Proarc's commitment to quality construction and timely delivery across the Emirates' education sector.

Info

  • Location: Hamidiya, Ajman
  • Total built-up area: 26,000 sqm
  • Start date: 05/02/2020
  • Completion: 28/07/2021
