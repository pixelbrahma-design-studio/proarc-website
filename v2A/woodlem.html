Woodlem Park Private School

Educational · UAE · 2017

Overview

Woodlem Park Private School is situated in Al-Jurf 3, Ajman, there are many neighboring ongoing developments that surrounds the school, making it an area envisioned for the future. Set over 2 floors, this school is designed to house a total of 4,230 students from KG to 12th standard. Equipped with state of the art auditorium, labs and library, this facility aims to be a safe hub for learning and growth for young students. Large, covered and open play areas separate for boys and girls, develops physical coordination, strength, and flexibility, as well as provides recreation and enjoyment for the children. The school is built in many phases for ease of construction. Design Intent

The architectural concept emerged from a careful reading of the site's orientation, prevailing wind patterns, and the client's vision for a landmark that would serve the community for decades. The massing strategy creates a dialogue between solid and void, allowing natural light to penetrate deep into the floor plates while maintaining thermal comfort in the UAE's demanding climate.

Material selections were chosen for their durability, low maintenance, and aesthetic contribution to the neighbourhood's evolving urban fabric.
