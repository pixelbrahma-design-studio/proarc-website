City School

Educational · UAE · 2016

Overview

City School is part of the City University campus in Al-Tallah, Ajman. This school equipped with the latest amenities has two wings. The playful and colorful nature of this project is seen through the color scheme used in the overall built form. The numerous geometric volumes add to the playfulness and create an interesting form. The faÃ§ade mimics exposed concrete panels to give it a rustic finish. The main entry is clearly defined using a projected volume in the center of the building.

Design Intent

The architectural concept emerged from a careful reading of the site's orientation, prevailing wind patterns, and the client's vision for a landmark that would serve the community for decades. The massing strategy creates a dialogue between solid and void, allowing natural light to penetrate deep into the floor plates while maintaining thermal comfort in the UAE's demanding climate.

Material selections were chosen for their durability, low maintenance, and aesthetic contribution to the neighbourhood's evolving urban fabric.
