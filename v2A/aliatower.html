Alia Tower

Residential · UAE · 2017

Overview

Alia Tower, is located on Mohammed bin Zayed Street behind Al Watan University in the Amra district of Ajman. Residents can choose from 252 contemporary apartment units; ranging from studio to two bedrooms in size. There are five parking levels with a podium housing the gym and swimming pool on the sixth floor. Commercial spaces are spread across the ground floor of the building. The overall built form is simplistic, creating a sense of balance using solid and translucent materials on the elevation. The solid materials reflect the exterior environment through the use of neutral and earthy tones.

Design Intent

The architectural concept emerged from a careful reading of the site's orientation, prevailing wind patterns, and the client's vision for a landmark that would serve the community for decades. The massing strategy creates a dialogue between solid and void, allowing natural light to penetrate deep into the floor plates while maintaining thermal comfort in the UAE's demanding climate.

Material selections were chosen for their durability, low maintenance, and aesthetic contribution to the neighbourhood's evolving urban fabric.
