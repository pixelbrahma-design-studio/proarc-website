Ajman Bank Headquaters

Ajman Bank headquarters is a G+M+7-storied iconic corporate complex located on Al-Ittihad Street adjacent to the Etisalat building. This HQ is responsible for entire transactions across the globe. The buildings bold, striking, reflective inclined glass façade creates many interlocking shapes and volumes which transcends the simple need of generating workspaces to become an Urban landmark.

Info

  • Client name: Ajman Bank
  • Total built-up area: 73,071 sq. ft.
  • Year of completion: 2015
