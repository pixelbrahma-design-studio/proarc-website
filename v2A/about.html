The Practice

Proarc

Proarc, one of the leading architectural practices in the UAE, has been an integral contributor to Ajman's emerging iconic skyline since 2006.

Over the past two decades we have pioneered a sustainable approach to architecture through a strikingly wide range of work — from urban masterplans and public infrastructure to retail spaces, healthcare facilities, and residential towers.

Based in Ajman with buildings across the Emirates, we have transformed our clients' businesses and missions, helping to make them leaders in their fields.
