Souk Salah

Home/ Projects/ Souk Salah
Overview

The recently expanded and refurbished Souk Salah adopts the traditional Arab architecture using wooden finish concrete columns and palm leaf ceiling to give a charming ambience with a blend of past and present. The souq is part of a heritage path leading from the Corniche to Ajman Museum. This busy commercial centre known for its traditional-styled buildings make it an ideal shopping stop for all local products and tailoring. The finer elements like the lighting, furniture and flooring add to the setting of this covered space taking you back to the enchanting old Arab souks, which was popular during that time.
