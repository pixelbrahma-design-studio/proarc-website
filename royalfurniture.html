Royal Furniture

Spanning a palatial 4,000 square meters of architectural grace, the Royal Furniture showroom stands as a luminous beacon of interior inspiration. This sophisticated Ground plus First Floor masterpiece is a symphony of light and space, designed to showcase the finest artistry in home décor across two expansive levels. Every corner of the gallery reflects a commitment to aesthetic excellence, offering a grand, multi-story journey through elegance and comfort. From its commanding street presence to its meticulously curated interiors, the project serves as a premier destination where visionary design meets the pinnacle of luxury living.

Info

  • Location: Al Jurf, Ajman
  • Total built-up area: 4,000 sqm
  • Structure: Ground + First Floor
