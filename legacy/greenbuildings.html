Green Buildings

This project features a series of five buildings (with built up area 9,383.36 sqm, 12,250 sqm, 6,841 sqm, 7,154 sqm, and 7,030 sqm), each designed with a ground floor dedicated to commercial use and a mezzanine plus two residential floors above. The ground-level commercial spaces are strategically planned to support a variety of businesses, providing convenient services to the local community. With around 74,000 square feet built up area.

Info

  • Client: Emirates Properties
  • Structure: G + M + 2 Floors
  • Buildings: 5 buildings
  • Total built-up area: ~74,000 sq. ft.
