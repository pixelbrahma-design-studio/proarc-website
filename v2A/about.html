The Practice

Proarc

Proarc, one of the leading architectural practices in the UAE, has been an integral contributor to Ajman's emerging iconic skyline since 2006. Our collaborative team of experienced architects, engineers, MEP engineers and site engineers ensure that all designs are executed with utmost thoroughness and commitment.

Over the past few years we have pioneered a sustainable approach to architecture through a strikingly wide range of work — from urban masterplans, public infrastructure, retail spaces, office spaces, health-care, institutional, mixed-use and skyscrapers to private houses. Our varied work allows us to take on any type and scale of project with a great deal of enthusiasm and understanding.

Based in Ajman, we have an international reputation, with buildings across the UAE. We have transformed our clients' businesses and missions, helping to make them leaders in their fields. We aim to create a lasting social, economic and environmental effect with everything that we take upon us.
