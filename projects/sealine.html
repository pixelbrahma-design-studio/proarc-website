Sealine Residences

Home/ Projects/ Sealine Residences
Overview

Currently rising in the heart of Al Zorah, Ajman, Sealine Residence is an upcoming architectural landmark located within Row 5 of the prestigious Beach Village Development. This sophisticated project spans over 28,000 square meters of meticulously planned built-up area, soaring nine floors above versatile lower ground levels. Once complete, the development will feature 99 exclusive apartments distributed across four distinct residential blocks, harmoniously integrated with over 500 square meters of premium commercial retail space. Designed to blend serene coastal beauty with modern urban convenience, Sealine Residence is set to redefine the skyline of Al Zorah.
