DECA 035 — Al Zorah Marina Residences

Residential · Marina Boardwalk, Al Zorah, Ajman
Home/ Projects/ DECA 035 — Al Zorah Marina Residences
Overview

This 2B+G+9-floor contemporary waterfront development stands along the Marina Boardwalk in Al Zorah, featuring a minimalist architectural language of soft neutral tones, glass façades, and curved balcony edges that open toward expansive marina views.

The building integrates modern residential living with active retail frontage, complemented by a central courtyard pool and a gym overlooking the water-inspired amenity space. Designed to blend comfort, lifestyle, and refined coastal aesthetics, the development contributes a modern landmark presence within the growing Al Zorah community.
