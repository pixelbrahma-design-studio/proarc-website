City University Campus

Educational · Ajman, UAE
Home/ Projects/ City University Campus
Overview

Located at the junction of Sheikh Ammar Road and Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed road, Ajman. Built in seven phases, this massive university campus is spread across an area of 1miliion square feet accommodating 11,000 students. The university consists of the state of the art auditorium, indoor play, swimming pool along with staff and student accommodation facilities, to be built in the later phases. Set over 3 floors, this purpose built educational facility will be built to house approximately 11,000 students from all fields of study and be a safe hub of learning and personal growth for them. Aesthetically engaging, regular geometric forms are utilized to create balance and convey a sense of effortless grace along the exterior faÃ§ade of the project. The exterior wall mimics exposed concrete panels which gives the overall built form a raw, rustic edge to it. The structure features neutral color tones and is reflective of its exterior environment. The use of natural lighting is abundant throughout the premises and strategically placed large openings provide ample light into the classrooms. Ample parking is provided on the ground and basement levels for ease of access.
