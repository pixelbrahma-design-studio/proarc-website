Al Ghala Mosque

Home/ Projects/ Al Ghala Mosque
Overview

Located in Al-Jurf this mosque takes inspiration from the traditional forms and elements of Islamic architecture, where colonnades and arches dominate creating a pleasing form. The structure uses natural and neutral color tones which is reflective of its exterior environment. This G+ mezzanine mosque located at the junction of a busy area creates a focal point for all worshippers to meet.
