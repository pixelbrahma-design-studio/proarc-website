CityLife, Al-Tallah

City life is located within the city University Campus in Al-Tallah, Ajman on Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road. This G+1 shopping mall uses a plethora of material to make a well-integrated building with a simple form. The use of exposed concrete panels, corten steel texture finish, off white Portuguese limestone and Aluminum louvers explores the various materials along the elevation.
