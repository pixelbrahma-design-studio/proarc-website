Azha Garden Residence

Home/ Projects/ Azha Garden Residence
Overview

Azha Garden Residence is a residential tower featuring a Ground Floor and four levels of dedicated parking. The development rises across 15 floors of high-quality living space with a built up area of 44,020 sqm, designed to meet the growing demand for modern housing in the region. Residents enjoy premium amenities, including a fully equipped Health Club and a versatile Roof area for recreation and community activities.
